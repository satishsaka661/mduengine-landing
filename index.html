The four questions we check

  • Sufficiency: is there enough conversion volume (around 50 a week) for the algorithm to learn?
  • Stability: does it hold day to day, or swing between great and dead days?
  • Consistency: are there days with real traffic but zero conversions, the sign of broken tracking?
  • Downside: how much spend sat on days the data couldn't be trusted?
Guidance only. Both tools are read-only: they never change campaigns or spend budget, and all decisions stay with the advertiser.

Two products, one principle: don't act on data that isn't ready.

Use Readiness before you hand budget to automation. Use the Decision Engine when you're deciding what to do with budget you're already spending.

How a Readiness check works

Connect, read-only

Log in with Meta and pick an ad account. We only request permission to read reports; we can't change anything.

We check the signal

Daily spend, conversions and clicks are scored on sufficiency, stability, consistency and downside, plus ad set fragmentation.

You get a verdict

A plain answer, the reason behind it, and the specific fix. The report data isn't stored after the check.

Who it's for

Performance marketers, in-house growth teams and agencies who want a defensible reason before they scale, and founders or finance teams who want to know whether the numbers behind a budget decision can be trusted.

Satish Saka

Founder · MDU Engine  |  Performance Marketing & MarTech Specialist

Satish Saka is a product founder and technology practitioner specialising in decision-support systems for data-driven environments. With over seven years of experience in performance marketing, attribution architecture, and growth strategy across India, Europe, Asia, and the US, he observed a consistent pattern: teams acting on data that was not yet ready — introducing instability rather than resolving it.

MDU Engine is the direct result of that observation. It introduces a structured evaluation layer between data observation and optimisation action — helping organisations assess whether their data is reliable enough to act on before any intervention is made. MDU Readiness brings the same thinking to Meta ad accounts: a read-only check of whether the conversion signal is strong enough to trust automated delivery. His research on this framework is published in JETIR and has received independent academic evaluation from KL University, Vijayawada.
