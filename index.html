Most wasted ad spend starts with data that wasn't ready.

Advantage+ and broad targeting optimise toward whatever signal you give them. These are the three ways that signal goes wrong, and MDU Readiness checks for each.

Too few conversions

Below roughly 50 conversions a week, the algorithm keeps guessing. Scaling budget at that point mostly scales the guessing.

Tracking that quietly breaks

A Pixel or Conversions API that stops firing for a few days looks like bad performance, and the algorithm learns the wrong lesson.

Results spread too thin

Enough conversions in total, split across so many ad sets that none of them ever leaves the learning phase.

Two tools, one rule: don't act on data that isn't ready.

Use Readiness before you hand budget to automation. Use the Decision Engine when you're deciding what to do with budget you already spend.

Can MDU Readiness change my campaigns or spend money?

No. It asks Meta only for read-only access (ads_read) to reports. It can't create, edit or pause ads, change budgets or spend anything.

What data do you read, and what do you keep?

Spend, impressions, clicks and conversions for the ad accounts you choose. We keep your connection and your account usage so you can run checks; the report data is used only to produce your result. We never sell or share it.

How do I delete my data?

Use "Delete my data" in the tool, or follow the steps on the data deletion page. It removes your connection, ad account access and account record.

What if my account doesn't have enough data?

You get a "Not enough data" result with what's missing, and that check isn't charged. A fair verdict needs at least 14 days with ads running.

Which ad platforms do you support?

MDU Readiness works with Meta Ads today; Google Ads support is next. The Decision Engine already works with Meta and Google Ads exports.

Is this for agencies too?

Yes. Connect any ad accounts your login can access and check each one. The Agency pack gives 40 checks.
